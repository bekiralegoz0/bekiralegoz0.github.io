How do I measure my heart rate?

Tap the measure button, then gently cover the rear camera and the flash with your fingertip. Keep your hand still, breathe normally and don't press too hard. A measurement takes 15 to 30 seconds.

Why didn't my measurement work?

  • Make sure your fingertip covers both the camera and the flash completely.
  • Rest your hand on a table so it doesn't move, and avoid pressing hard.
  • Cold fingers can give a weak signal; warm your hands and try again.
  • Check that Heart Tracker is allowed to use the camera in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.

Does the app measure blood pressure or blood sugar?

No. Heart Tracker measures heart rate and heart rate variability with your phone camera. Blood pressure and blood sugar are logs: you record the readings you take with your own monitor or glucose meter, and the app shows your trends.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Heart Tracker. You can change or cancel your plan there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal.

I bought Premium but it isn't active

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile › Delete my account. This permanently deletes your measurements, logs, medications and profile from your iPhone. Your subscription is not affected. Readings you saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can delete them yourself.
