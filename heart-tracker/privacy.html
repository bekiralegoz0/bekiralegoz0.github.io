Heart Tracker: BP & Blood Sugar

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

The short version

  • Your health readings stay on your device. There is no account and no Heart Tracker server that stores them.
  • Camera images used to measure your heart rate are processed on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share health data with advertisers or analytics services.
  • We use a few services to understand how the app is used, to measure our ads and to process subscriptions. They receive usage information linked to a random ID, not your health readings.

Who we are

Heart Tracker: BP & Blood Sugar (“Heart Tracker”, “the app”, “we”) is developed by Bekir, an independent developer, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy. You can reach us at edadeveloper0@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The following information is saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We do not have access to it.

  • Heart rate measurements and the values calculated from them (such as HRV, stress, energy and heart score).
  • Readings you log: blood pressure, blood sugar, blood oxygen and water intake.
  • Medications and reminder times you add, habits and badges.
  • Your age and sex, which you enter during setup to personalise results.
  • Answers to the in-app health tests.

This information is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup, which Apple or you control.

Camera

To measure your heart rate, the app uses the rear camera and flash while your fingertip covers the lens. The images are analysed on your device in real time and are never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads steps, active energy, resting heart rate, weight, height and sleep to show them next to your measurements, and can save your heart rate, HRV, blood pressure and blood oxygen readings to Health. You choose which types to allow and can change this at any time in the Health app. Data from Apple Health stays on your device, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with third parties.

Notifications

Medication and habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder content is sent to us.

Information that leaves your device

Product analytics (Mixpanel)

We use Mixpanel, with data stored in the European Union, to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Mixpanel receives:

  • A random identifier created when you install the app. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and a new one is created if you reinstall.
  • App usage events, for example which screens you open, which features you use, steps of the setup, paywall views and subscription events.
  • General information such as your age group, whether you have Premium, app version, device model, operating system, language and approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address.

Events may say that a reading was taken or was in a category such as “normal” or “high”, but never contain the reading itself: no heart rate, HRV, blood pressure, blood sugar, blood oxygen or water amounts, and no health test answers.

Advertising measurement (Meta)

We use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure how well our ads work. It receives a limited set of events: app installs and opens, completing setup, viewing the subscription screen and starting a purchase, together with the random identifier above and an anonymous ID created by Meta's SDK. Purchases may be reported to Meta by RevenueCat (see below).

Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Meta only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. No health information is ever sent to Meta.

Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. It receives your purchase and subscription status, the random identifier above, and technical information about your device and App Store account needed to validate purchases. RevenueCat may forward purchase events (such as a started trial, purchase or renewal) to Mixpanel and Meta so we can measure subscriptions and ads.

Medication search

When you search for a medication name, the text you type is sent to the free Clinical Tables service of the U.S. National Library of Medicine to suggest names. No identifier or other information from the app is sent with the search.

How we use information

  • To provide the app's features on your device.
  • To understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve it.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising.
  • To provide and manage subscriptions.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) applies, we rely on your consent (for tracking and Apple Health), on performing our contract with you (subscriptions) and on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app (analytics).

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers named above, who process it on our behalf, and when required by law. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, with appropriate safeguards.

Retention

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or the app. Analytics data in Mixpanel is kept only as long as needed to understand and improve the app, and is then deleted. Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the subscription and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use the app without connecting Apple Health and without allowing tracking.
  • You can delete your data on the device at any time by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, email us at edadeveloper0@gmail.com.

Children

Heart Tracker is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Heart Tracker is designed for general wellness and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always consult a healthcare professional about your health.
